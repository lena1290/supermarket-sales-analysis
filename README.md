# Supermarket Sales Analysis (Jan–Mar 2019)

Analysis of 1,000 supermarket transactions across three branches — from raw data to an interactive dashboard.

**Tools:** Excel (Power Query, PivotTables) · Tableau

## Pipeline

Excel (cleaning and transformation) → PivotTables (validation) → Tableau (visualization)

## Key Findings

- Total sales were 322,967 from 1,000 transactions, generating 15,379 in gross income.
- Giza was the top branch by sales (110,569), ahead of Alex (106,200) and Cairo (106,198). The gap between Alex and Cairo is under 3 in sales, so the branches are very evenly matched.
- Food and beverages led all product lines (56,145), while Health and beauty trailed (49,194) with the fewest transactions (152).
- Ewallet (345) and Cash (344) were used in almost equal numbers of transactions, with Credit card behind at 311.
- Data was complete and consistent: no blanks, no duplicate invoice IDs, and all cost, tax and total calculations reconciled.
